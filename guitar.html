GUITAR • LESSON 1

Your First Guitar Lesson

Learn how to hold your guitar, place your fingers, and play your first chords.

01

How to Hold Your Guitar

Sit comfortably and rest the body of the guitar on your leg. Keep the neck pointing slightly upward instead of completely flat.

Tip

Relax your shoulders and arms. You should be able to reach the strings without stretching.

02

Learn the Strings

A standard guitar has six strings. From the thickest string to the thinnest:

6 E
5 A
4 D
3 G
2 B
1 E
03

Your First Chords

We're starting with three chords:

Remember

Press the strings firmly enough to make a clear sound, but don't squeeze too hard.

04

Your First Chord Progression

Now let's connect the chords together. Play each chord once before moving to the next.

G C G C G D G

G → C → G → C → G → D → G

PRACTICE

Your Mission

Try switching between G, C and D without stopping. Don't worry about speed yet. Focus on making each chord sound clear.
