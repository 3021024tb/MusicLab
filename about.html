ABOUT MUSICLAB

Music should be fun to learn.

MusicLab is a free music-learning platform built to make learning instruments and music theory simple, enjoyable, and accessible.

THE STORY

How MusicLab Started

MusicLab started with a simple question: what should I make?

A game? Nah.

After asking my dad, he suggested making a music-learning platform.

So I started building MusicLab. What began as a small project has grown into something I want to keep developing for years.

THE MISSION

Learn without the pressure.

MusicLab is designed to help people learn without feeling judged or overwhelmed. You can learn at your own speed and come back whenever you're ready.

ALWAYS FREE

MusicLab is made for everyone.

The goal is to keep the core learning experience free so anyone can learn music without needing to worry about a paywall.

THE FUTURE

This is only the beginning.

MusicLab will keep growing as more lessons, instruments, tools, and features are added.

The goal isn't to build everything at once. It's to build it properly, one lesson at a time.
